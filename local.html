Local SEO, maps and Google Business Profile — in one audit

Built for brick-and-mortar, service-area and multi-location businesses. ./seo local <url> checks GBP health, NAP consistency, citations, review signals, local schema and location-page quality — then gives you the map-pack levers that actually move the needle.

What it covers

  • Google Business Profile — status, category, hours, photos.
  • NAP consistency — name / address / phone across the web.
  • Citation health — directories and platforms.
  • Review signals — volume, recency, text, rating.
  • Local schema — LocalBusiness/JSON-LD correctness.
  • Location pages — quality, unique content, multi-location structure.
  • Business type detection — brick-and-mortar vs SAB vs hybrid, plus the vertical.

Run it

./seo local https://example.com
./seo maps --grid "83026 Rosenheim"     # geo-grid / GBP audit

FAQ

Reviews matter?
Reviews weigh heavily in the map pack — a 10-review threshold is the practical bar to clear, and text + recency matter as much as the count.
Is it for single-location or multi-location?
Both. It detects the business type and flags when a multi-location setup risks thin, duplicate content.
30+ location pages?
seo-audit warns (enforce 60% unique content) and hard-stops at 50+.
