Get cited by AI search, not just ranked by it

Search is becoming generative. ./seo geo <url> — plus the GEO sub-skill — optimizes your content for AI Overviews, ChatGPT web search, Perplexity and other AI-powered search experiences, anchored in Google's own AI optimization guidance.

What it checks

  • Brand mention signals — how often and how consistently you're referenced.
  • AI crawler accessibility — can the content be read by AI agents?
  • llms.txt compliance — the AI-facing index file.
  • Passage-level citability — are discrete, citable answers extractable?
  • Platform-specific optimization — ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Llama/Gemini surfaces.

Run it

./seo geo https://example.com
./seo content https://example.com      # with AI-citation-readiness scoring

FAQ

What is GEO?
Generative Engine Optimization — making content readable and citable by AI answer engines, not just ranking in traditional SERPs.
What's the primary source?
Google's own AI optimization guidance under Search Central, plus platform-specific behavior for ChatGPT/Perplexity.
Is this just SEO with a new name?
No — it adds the citable-answer and AI-crawler-accessibility layer that classic SEO misses.
