Content that earns trust — and AI citations

./seo content <url|file> scores content against Google's quality guidance, with an AI-citation-readiness dimension built in. It runs the canonical Who/How/Why heuristic before scoring E-E-A-T sub-factors, and flags thin content, readability and depth issues.

The anchor: Google's Who / How / Why test

QuestionWhat to look for
WhoClear authorship and entity behind the content.
HowEvidence of experience and first-hand information.
WhyA clear, beneficial reason for the page to exist.

Every page audit passes this before E-E-A-T sub-factor scoring.

Run it

./seo content https://example.com
./seo content page.html
./seo content-brief "bavarian restaurant rosenheim"  # brief from a topic

FAQ

E-E-A-T vs AI citation readiness?
seo-audit treats them together — experience/authority/expertise/trust is also what makes a passage citable by an AI engine.
Does it need an API key for readability?
No. Content scoring runs locally and keylessly.
Can it work on a file, not a URL?
Yes — pass a local file as well as a URL.
